# Тоғызқұмалақ — Official Rules

**Source:** World Togyzqumalak Federation / World Nomad Games Rules
**Source URL:** https://worldnomadgames.kz/en/page/Togyzkumalak (Referenced by World Togyzqumalak Federation)
**Date Checked:** 2026-08-14

## Overview
Togyzqumalak (Тоғызқұмалақ) is a traditional Kazakh board game from the mancala family. It is a game of logic and strategy. The objective is to collect more than 81 stones (qūmalaq) into your storage well (qazan).

## Board Setup
- The board consists of two rows of 9 small holes called **otau** (отау).
- There are two large storage holes called **qazan** (қазан).
- At the start, each of the 18 otau contains 9 stones (qūmalaq).
- Total stones in the game: 18 * 9 = 162 stones.

## Moving (Көшу)
- Players take turns moving stones.
- A player chooses one of their non-empty otaus.
- If the chosen otau has **only 1 stone**, the player simply moves that stone to the next adjacent otau counter-clockwise.
- If the chosen otau has **more than 1 stone**, the player takes all stones from it. They leave 1 stone in the starting otau, and sow the rest of the stones, one by one, into the consecutive otaus in a counter-clockwise direction.
- Sowing can continue onto the opponent's side and back to the player's side.

## Capturing (Ату)
- A capture occurs if the **last stone** sown falls into an **opponent's otau** AND makes the total number of stones in that otau **even** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, etc.).
- If this happens, all stones from that otau are immediately captured and moved to the capturing player's qazan.
- If the last stone lands in an opponent's otau but makes the number odd, no capture is made.
- If the last stone lands in the player's own otau (regardless of even/odd), no capture is made.

## Tuzdyk (Тұздық)
Tuzdyk is a special rule that allows a player to convert an opponent's otau into their own "sacred well".
- A player can declare an opponent's otau a Tuzdyk if the **last stone** sown falls into that otau AND makes the total number of stones exactly **3**.
- When an otau becomes a Tuzdyk, the 3 stones in it are captured by the player and placed in their qazan.
- For the rest of the game, any stone that falls into this Tuzdyk immediately goes into the qazan of the player who created the Tuzdyk.

### Restrictions on Tuzdyk
1. A player can have **only one** Tuzdyk per game.
2. The 9th otau of an opponent cannot be turned into a Tuzdyk.
3. A Tuzdyk cannot be created symmetrically to the opponent's Tuzdyk. That is, if the opponent has already created a Tuzdyk in your N-th otau, you cannot create a Tuzdyk in their N-th otau. (Otaus are numbered 1 to 9 from left to right for each player).

## Atsyrau (Атсырау)
- If it is a player's turn but all their otaus are empty, they cannot make a move. This situation is called **Atsyrau**.
- When this happens, the game ends immediately.
- The other player collects all the remaining stones from their own otaus and places them into their qazan.

## Winning the Game
- The game ends when one player collects **82 or more stones** in their qazan. This player is the winner.
- If both players collect exactly **81 stones**, the game is a **draw**.
- The game can also end via Atsyrau, after which stones are counted.
